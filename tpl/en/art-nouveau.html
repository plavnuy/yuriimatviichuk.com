At the outset, my brief for this interior was simply to work out the colour scheme for the walls.

I proposed something more complete: alongside the wall colours, a toning of the stucco moulding and a small mural — some 8 square metres — painted over gold acrylic.

It all took seven months. The artists Valeriy Vasiliev and Yelena Solodianikova worked with me.

We remember the kind and charming owner of this house warmly.
